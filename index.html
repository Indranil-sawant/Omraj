Do I need to provide cleaning supplies or equipment? expand_more
No, absolutely not! Our team comes fully equipped with heavy-duty machines, vacuums, and eco-friendly cleaning chemicals. You don't need to provide anything except water and electricity access.
How long does a deep home cleaning take? expand_more
A standard 2BHK flat in Kolhapur typically takes about 4 to 6 hours with a team of 3 professionals. The exact time depends on the current condition and size of the property.
Are your cleaners from Kolhapur? Are they verified? expand_more
Yes, all our staff members are local to Kolhapur and nearby areas. They undergo a strict background check, address verification, and intensive training before they ever step into a client's home.

Yes! If you have guests arriving or need urgent post-party cleanup, we offer same-day emergency services subject to team availability. Call us directly for immediate booking.
