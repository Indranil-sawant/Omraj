Our Story

Founded in the heart of Kolhapur, Omraj Cleaning Services started with a simple mission: to provide families and businesses with a level of cleanliness that feels truly premium, without the premium hassle.

We noticed that local residents in areas like Tarabai Park and Rajarampuri struggled to find reliable, professional, and well-equipped cleaners. We stepped in to fill that gap. Today, we are proud to be the #1 choice for over 5,000 households across Kolhapur, Ichalkaranji, and Sangli.
